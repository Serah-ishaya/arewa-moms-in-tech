# Contributing to Arewa Moms in Tech

Thank you for your interest in supporting Arewa Moms in Tech Initiative.

At this early stage, contributions can include:

- Mentorship
- Digital-skills training
- Community research
- Storytelling
- Technical support
- Partnerships
- Program ideas
- Opportunity sharing

## How to contribute

1. Fork the repository.
2. Create a new branch.
3. Make your changes.
4. Commit your work.
5. Open a Pull Request explaining your contribution.

All contributions should be respectful, practical, inclusive, and centered on the needs and dignity of mothers across Northern Nigeria.
