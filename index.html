AREWA MOMS IN TECH INITIATIVE

Motherhood should not be a barrier to opportunity.

We are building a community that helps stay-at-home mothers across Northern Nigeria learn digital skills, access opportunities, find mentors and build sustainable careers around their realities.

Explore the Initiative

OUR PURPOSE

Building pathways from home to the digital economy.

Arewa Moms in Tech Initiative is a proposed future NGO focused on digital inclusion, mentorship, opportunity access, storytelling and economic empowerment for stay-at-home mothers across Northern Nigeria.

Our Vision

A Northern Nigeria where every stay-at-home mother has the skills, confidence and support to participate in the digital economy.

Our Mission

To equip mothers with practical skills, mentorship, technology access and a strong community.

Our Focus

Flexible learning and opportunity pathways that recognize childcare, family responsibilities and different starting points.

OUR PROGRAMS

Five pathways to participation.

01

Digital Skills Academy

Practical learning in customer support, virtual assistance, product management, digital marketing, design, AI, content creation and data.

02

Mom-to-Mom Mentorship

Connect beginners with experienced women who can provide guidance, encouragement and career insight.

03

Voices of Arewa Moms

Document real stories of mothers learning, working, creating businesses and navigating technology.

04

Opportunity Connect

Share jobs, remote work, internships, fellowships, scholarships, grants and training opportunities.

05

Community Learning Hubs

Create online and physical spaces where mothers can learn, collaborate, network and support one another.

THE ROAD AHEAD

From community idea to sustainable NGO.

01 — Listen

Speak with mothers across Northern Nigeria and understand their needs.

02 — Train

Launch accessible digital-skills sessions.

03 — Connect

Build mentor relationships and opportunity pathways.

04 — Tell

Share stories that make the journeys of Northern mothers visible.

05 — Grow

Develop partnerships, funding, chapters and formal NGO structures.

10,000 mothers is the long-term goal.

We envision a network reaching communities across all 19 Northern states and creating practical pathways into the digital economy.
