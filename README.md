
# Arewa Moms in Tech Initiative (AMTI)

> Empowering stay-at-home mothers across Northern Nigeria to participate in the digital economy.

## About

Arewa Moms in Tech Initiative (AMTI) is a proposed future NGO/community initiative focused on helping stay-at-home mothers across Northern Nigeria gain digital skills, access technology opportunities, build supportive networks, and pursue income-generating careers while managing family responsibilities.

The initiative combines:

- Digital skills training
- Mentorship
- Opportunity sharing
- Storytelling
- Community building
- Entrepreneurship support
- Advocacy for digital inclusion

## Vision

A Northern Nigeria where every stay-at-home mother has the opportunity, skills, confidence, and support to participate in the digital economy, achieve financial independence, and contribute meaningfully to her family and community.

## Mission

To empower stay-at-home mothers across Northern Nigeria through digital skills development, mentorship, technology access, entrepreneurship support, and community building.

## Our Programs

### 1. Digital Skills Academy

Training in:

- Customer Support
- Virtual Assistance
- Product Management
- Digital Marketing
- Graphic Design
- Content Creation
- Artificial Intelligence
- Prompt Engineering
- Data Analysis

### 2. Mom-to-Mom Mentorship

Connecting beginners with experienced women in technology, business, and digital careers.

### 3. Voices of Arewa Moms

A storytelling platform documenting the experiences of mothers learning technology, building businesses, finding remote work, and balancing family responsibilities.

### 4. Opportunity Connect

A platform for sharing:

- Jobs
- Remote work
- Internships
- Fellowships
- Scholarships
- Grants
- Training opportunities

### 5. Community Learning Hubs

Online and physical communities where mothers can learn, collaborate, network, and support one another.

## Target Beneficiaries

AMTI primarily serves:

- Stay-at-home mothers
- New mothers returning to work
- Women transitioning into technology
- Women seeking remote careers
- Female graduates facing barriers to traditional employment
- Mothers interested in digital entrepreneurship

## Geographic Focus

Northern Nigeria, with a long-term goal of reaching communities across all 19 Northern states.

## First-Year Roadmap

### Phase 1 — Listen

Talk to mothers across Northern Nigeria and understand their needs, interests, skills, and challenges.

### Phase 2 — Train

Launch monthly digital-skills sessions and beginner-friendly learning pathways.

### Phase 3 — Connect

Build mentorship relationships and connect participants to relevant opportunities.

### Phase 4 — Tell

Share the stories and experiences of Northern mothers in technology.

### Phase 5 — Grow

Develop partnerships, sponsorships, funding opportunities, and community chapters.

## Long-Term Goals

Over 5–10 years, AMTI aims to:

- Establish chapters across Northern Nigeria.
- Train at least 10,000 mothers.
- Build a strong network of women mentors.
- Connect participants with employment and entrepreneurship opportunities.
- Promote digital inclusion.
- Become a recognized platform for mothers participating in the digital economy.

## Impact Measurement

Potential indicators include:

- Number of mothers enrolled
- Number completing training
- Number receiving certifications
- Number matched with mentors
- Number accessing jobs or freelance opportunities
- Number starting digital businesses
- Number of states reached
- Number of community sessions held
